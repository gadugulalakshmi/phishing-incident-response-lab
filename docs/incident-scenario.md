# Incident Response Scenario

## Incident Title
Phishing Attack Leading to Account Compromise

## Organization
Acme Corp

## Severity
High

## Incident Summary
An employee received a phishing email that appeared to be from Microsoft.
The email contained a link asking the employee to verify their account.

The employee clicked the link and entered their credentials on the fake
login page.

Shortly afterward, the security team identified suspicious login activity
from an unusual location.

## Initial Indicators
- Suspicious phishing email
- Malicious URL
- Compromised user credentials
- Unusual login location
- Suspicious account activity

## Potential Impact
- Unauthorized access to the employee account
- Exposure of sensitive information
- Possible lateral movement
- Potential data theft

## Initial Response Objective
The security team must investigate the incident, contain the compromised
account, remove the threat, restore normal operations, and document lessons learned.
